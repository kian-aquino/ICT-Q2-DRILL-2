Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center



K-12 learning, with 5 different campuses, namely: Sta. Ana, Angeles, Greenhills, Las Pinas, and Fairview.

We have different academic and non-academic clubs including Basketball and Volleyball varsity teams, Robotics, Communications, Social Studies, Science, COCC, and more. We also offer multiple subjects aside from academic ones including TLE, ICT, PEH, Music and Arts, and VE.

Here, we value learning and see it as an essential part of life. Our mission is to teach and nurture are students as they are the future of our country.
